# Summary: U-17_0002_r (1291, Säckingen)

Conrad, Ulrich, John, and Werner of Reitnau sold a hereditary tithe in Othmarsingen, Hendschiken, and Dottikon to Arnold Trutman of Beromünster for 102 pounds of pennies. Adelheid of Kaiserstuhl, canoness of Säckingen (diocese of Constance), consented, and Arnold or his heirs must pay Säckingen church ten modii of rye yearly by Zurich measure. The brothers promised warranty, and the Säckingen chapter sealed the charter before witnesses. This was enacted in the cloister of Säckingen on 24 March 1291.
