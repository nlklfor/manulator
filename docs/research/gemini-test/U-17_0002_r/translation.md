# Translation: U-17_0002_r (1291, Säckingen)

Gemini answer, one entry per sentence

## 1

Universis et singulis presentem litteram inspecturis vel etiam audituris nos Chuͦnradus, Ulricus, Iohannes et Wernherus, fratres carnales dicti de Reitnowa, subscriptorum notitiam cum salute.

To all and each who shall inspect or also hear the present letter, we, Conrad, Ulrich, John, and Werner, full brothers called of Reitnau, send knowledge of the underwritten matters with greeting.

## 2

ne gesta vel contractus bone fidei inter homines initos prudentia mediante longevo decursu temporis per maliciam hominum, qui ad hanc ab initio temporis inclinantur, valeant enervari, expedit ipsa litterarum testimoniis stabiliri.

Lest deeds or contracts entered into in good faith among men with the mediation of prudence might, through the long lapse of time, be weakened by the malice of men, who are inclined to this from the beginning of time, it is expedient that they be established by the testimonies of letters.

## 3

noverint igitur universi et singuli, quorum interest vel intererit, quod nos predicti Chuͦnradus, Ulricus, Iohannes et Wernherus, fratres dicti de Reitnowa, discreto viro Arnoldo dicto Trutman, villano seu oppidano Beronensi, decimam in villis videlicet in Othwessingen, in Henschinchon et in Dothinchon sitam, ad nos iure hereditario ab ecclesia Seconiensi Constantiensis dyocesis pertinentem, pro centum et duabus libris denariorum usualis monete, quas libras denariorum ad plenum et in toto profitemur nos presentibus recepisse, consensu et assense honorabilis domine Adelheidis dicte de Cheiserstuͦl, canonice ecclesie Secuniensis, tunc procuratricis eiusdem decime, expresso accedente, ad cuius manum ipsam predictam decimam resignavimus, iusto venditionis titulo tradidimus et presentibus tradidimus iure prehabito hereditario videlicet possidendam omne ius, quod nobis vel pueris nostris seu proli nostre in dicta decima competebat vel competere videbatur, ad eundem Arnoldum universaliter et specialiter transferendo, ita tamen, quod dictus Arnoldus vel quicumque suorum heredum sepedictam decimam possederit, singulis annis decem modios siliginis predicte ecclesie Seconinensis nomine census mensure Thuricensis solvere tenebitur seu etiam expedire.

Therefore, let all and each whom it concerns or shall concern know that we, the aforesaid Conrad, Ulrich, John, and Werner, brothers called of Reitnau, have handed over and by these presents do hand over by a just title of sale—namely to be possessed by the aforementioned hereditary right—to the discreet man Arnold called Trutman, villager or townsman of Beromünster, the tithe situated in the villages of Othmarsingen, Hendschiken, and Dottikon, belonging to us by hereditary right from the church of Säckingen in the Diocese of Constance, for one hundred and two pounds of pennies of the usual currency, which pounds of pennies we acknowledge by these presents to have received in full and in whole, with the express consent and assent of the honorable lady Adelheid called of Kaiserstuhl, canoness of the church of Säckingen, then procuratrix of the same tithe, into whose hand we resigned the aforesaid tithe, transferring universally and specially to the same Arnold all right which belonged or seemed to belong to us or our children or our offspring in the said tithe, on condition however that the said Arnold, or whichever of his heirs shall possess the oft-mentioned tithe, shall be bound to pay or deliver every year ten bushels of rye of Zurich measure to the aforesaid church of Säckingen by way of rent.

## 4

promittimus etiam presentibus pro nobis nostrisque successoribus, quandocumque necesse fuerit, omni loco de dicta decima ipsi vel cuius seu quorum interest vel intererit prestare debitam warandiam.

We also promise by these presents, for ourselves and our successors, whenever it shall be necessary, in every place to provide due warranty concerning the said tithe to him or to him or those whom it concerns or shall concern.

## 5

ut autem decima predicta venditio in firmento sui roboris perpetuo valeat permanere, devotis nostris petitionibus optinuimus sigillo capituli Secuniensis ecclesie dicte dyocesis hoc instrumentum seu presentem paginam fideliter.

Moreover, in order that the aforesaid sale of the tithe may remain perpetually in the firmness of its strength, through our devout petitions we have obtained that this instrument or present page be faithfully confirmed with the seal of the chapter of the church of Säckingen of the said diocese.

## 6

testes rogati, qui resignationi ac concessioni interfuerunt sunt hii: Gerungus, dyaconus Beronensis, Iohannes de Urberch, Ruodolfus Sartor, Ernwinus et Heinricus filius suus, cives Secunienses.

The requested witnesses who were present at the resignation and grant are these: Gerung, deacon of Beromünster, John of Urberch, Rudolf Sartor, Ernwin and Henry his son, citizens of Säckingen.

## 7

nos capitulum antedictum in recognitionem et ratihabitionem omnium premissorum ob interventum rogatuum premissorum fratrum scilicet Chuͦnradi, Ulrici, Iohannis et Wernheri devotorum sigillum nostrum presentibus duximus appendendum.

We, the aforesaid chapter, in recognition and ratification of all the foregoing, on account of the intervention of the devout requests of the aforesaid brothers, namely Conrad, Ulrich, John, and Werner, have seen fit to append our seal to these presents.

## 8

datum et tracta sunt hec anno domini mͦ ccͦ lxxxͦxiͦ in claustro Seconiensi viiij kalendas aprilis, indictione iiij̃.

Given and transacted in the year of the Lord 1291 in the cloister of Säckingen on the ninth day before the Kalends of April, in the fourth indiction.
