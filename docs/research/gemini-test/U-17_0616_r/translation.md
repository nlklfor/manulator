# Translation: U-17_0616_r (1442, Basel)

Gemini answer, one entry per sentence

## 1

Sacrosancta generalis synodus Basiliensis in spiritu sancto legitime congregata universalem ecclesiam representans dilecto ecclesie filio preposito ecclesie sancti Petri Basiliensis salutem et omnipotentis dei benedictionem.

The most holy general synod of Basel, lawfully assembled in the Holy Spirit and representing the universal Church, to the beloved son of the Church, the provost of the church of Saint Peter in Basel, greeting and the blessing of Almighty God.

## 2

sacre religionis sub qua dilecte in Christo ecclesie filie abbatissa et conventus monasterii Sancte Clare in Camporegis ordinis dicte sancte Constanciensis diocesis mundanis abiectis illecebris sub regularis vite sanctimonia et in humilitatis spiritu devotum et sedulum exhibent domino famulatum promeretur honestas ut votis earum illis presertim per que dicti monasterii illiusque personarum necessitatibus subvenitur favorabiliter annuamus.

The integrity of the sacred religious life—under which our beloved daughters of the Church in Christ, the abbess and convent of the monastery of Saint Clare in Königsfelden, of the order of the said saint, in the Diocese of Constance, having cast aside worldly allurements, render devoted and diligent service to the Lord under the sanctity of a regular life and in a spirit of humility—merits that we favorably assent to their wishes, especially those by which the needs of the said monastery and of its members are relieved.

## 3

exhibita siquidem nobis nuper pro parte earundem abbatisse et conventus petitio continebat quod dudum carissimus ecclesie filius Fredericus Romanorum rex illustris singularis devotionis fervore pro sue suorumque progenitorum et posterorum animarum salute inter alia iuspatronatus parrochialis ecclesie in Schintznach Basiliensis diocesis quod ad ipsum regem Fredericum ratione comitatus sui de Haspurg legitime pertinebat eisdem abbatisse et conventui pia largitione donavit ac etiam concessit prout in litteris desuper confectis ipsius Frederici regis sigillo munitis dicitur plenius contineri ipseque abbatissa et conventus donationis huiusmodi vigore possessionem vel quasi pacificam iurispatronatus huiusmodi extiterunt assecute et sicut eadem petitio subiungebat si dicta ecclesia prefato monasterio in quo etiam absque aliis gravaminibus et oneribus illi dietim incumbentibus centum persone vel circa continuo alimentari habent pro illius abbatisse et monialium necessitatibus ac gravissimis que illis incumbunt oneribus huiusmodi supportandis necnon maxima hospitalitate tam nobilium quam aliorum ac etiam pauperum peregrinorum ad ipsum monasterium etiam numero non mediocriter copioso confluentium tenenda uniretur annecteretur et incorporaretur.

Indeed, a petition recently presented to us on behalf of the same abbess and convent contained that some time ago the most dear son of the Church, Frederick, illustrious King of the Romans, out of the fervor of singular devotion and for the salvation of his own soul and the souls of his ancestors and descendants, gave and also granted by pious bounty to the same abbess and convent, among other things, the right of patronage of the parish church in Schinznach in the Diocese of Basel, which lawfully belonged to the same King Frederick by reason of his County of Habsburg, as is said to be more fully contained in the letters drawn up thereon and fortified with the seal of the same King Frederick; and the same abbess and convent, by virtue of this donation, obtained peaceful possession, or quasi-possession, of this right of patronage; and, as the same petition added, if the said church were united, annexed, and incorporated into the aforesaid monastery—in which, even apart from other hardships and burdens resting upon it daily, about a hundred persons must continually be fed—for relieving the needs of its abbess and nuns and supporting these very heavy burdens resting upon them, as well as for maintaining the very great hospitality toward both nobles and others, and also poor pilgrims flocking to the same monastery in no small number;

## 4

ex hoc abbatissa pro tempore existens et moniales huiusmodi decentius sustentari et divinis laudibus sub regularis vite sanctimonia quietius intendere hospitalitatem quoque inibi teneri solitam melius tenere et conservare ac incumbentia eis onera huiusmodi commodius supportare valerent tempore procedente.

as a result of this, the abbess for the time being and the said nuns would be able as time goes on to be more fittingly sustained, to devote themselves more peacefully to divine praises under the sanctity of a regular life, also to better maintain and preserve the hospitality accustomed to be kept therein, and more conveniently to bear these burdens resting upon them.

## 5

quare pro parte abbatisse et conventus predictorum nobis fuit humiliter supplicatum ut ipsis earumque monasterio in premissis oportune considere de universalis ecclesie clementia dignaremur.

Wherefore, on behalf of the aforesaid abbess and convent, it was humbly supplicated to us that we would deign out of the clemency of the universal Church to make suitable provision for them and their monastery in the aforesaid matters.

## 6

nos igitur de premissis certam notitiam non habentes huiusmodi quoque supplicationibus inclinati discretioni tue per hec scripta committimus et mandamus quatenus vocatis venerabili episcopo Basiliensi necnon rectore dicte parrochialis ecclesie et aliis qui fuerint evocandi super premissis auctoritate nostra te diligenter informes et si per eandem informationem reppereris ita esse super quo tuam conscientiam oneramus dictam ecclesiam cuius decem et septem cum omnibus iuribus et pertinentiis suis monasterio prefato cuius ducentarum marcharum argenti fructus redditus et proventus secundum communem extimationem valorem annuum ut ipse abbatissa et conventus asserunt non excedunt eadem auctoritate nostra imperpetuum unias incorpores et annectas ita quod cedente vel decedente prefato moderno rectore eiusdem ecclesie seu illam alias quomodolibet dimittente liceat extunc eisdem abbatisse et conventui per se vel alium seu alios corporalem possessionem ecclesie iuriumque et pertinentiarum predictorum auctoritate propria libere apprehendere illiusque fructus redditus et proventus huiusmodi reservata tamen de illis pro perpetuo vicario in eadem ecclesia instituendo septem marcharum argenti seu alias congrua portione de qua idem vicarius sive rector decenter sustentari episcopalia et iura alia solvere ac sibi incumbentia onera supportare possit in suos ac monasterii et ecclesie predictorum usus et utilitates convertere et perpetuo retinere alicuius alterius super hoc licentia minime requisita non obstantibus constitutionibus et ordinationibus apostolicis necnon statutis et consuetudinibus monasterii et ordinis predictorum etiam iuramento confirmatione apostolica vel quavis firmitate alia roboratis ceterisque contrariis quibuscunque aut si aliqui super provisionibus sibi faciendis de huiusmodi vel aliis beneficiis ecclesiasticis in illis partibus speciales vel generales apostolice sedis vel legatorum eius litteras impetrarint etiam si per eas ad inhibitionem reservationem et decretum vel alias quomodolibet sit processum.

We therefore, not having certain knowledge of the aforesaid matters, and inclined to these supplications, by these writings commit to and command your discretion that, having summoned the venerable Bishop of Basel as well as the rector of the said parish church and others who ought to be summoned, you diligently inform yourself by our authority concerning the aforesaid matters, and if through the same inquiry you find it to be so—upon which we burden your conscience—you unite, incorporate, and annex forever by our same authority the said church, whose [value is] seventeen [marks], with all its rights and appurtenances, to the aforesaid monastery, whose fruits, rents, and revenues, as the same abbess and convent assert, do not exceed the annual value of two hundred marks of silver according to the common estimation; so that, upon the resignation or death of the aforesaid present rector of the same church, or upon his relinquishing it in any other way whatsoever, it shall be lawful from then on for the same abbess and convent, by themselves or by another or others, freely to take corporeal possession of the aforesaid church, rights, and appurtenances by their own authority, and to convert its fruits, rents, and revenues to their own uses and benefits and those of the aforesaid monastery and church and retain them forever—reserving, however, from them for a perpetual vicar to be instituted in the same church a fitting portion of seven marks of silver or otherwise, from which the same vicar or rector may be fittingly sustained, pay the episcopal and other dues, and bear the burdens incumbent upon him—without the license of any other person being in any way required hereupon; notwithstanding apostolic constitutions and ordinances, as well as the statutes and customs of the aforesaid monastery and order, even those strengthened by oath, apostolic confirmation, or any other security, and any other things whatsoever to the contrary; or if any persons have obtained special or general letters of the Apostolic See or of its legates concerning provisions to be made to them of such or other ecclesiastical benefices in those parts, even if proceedings have been taken by them to inhibition, reservation, and decree, or in any other way whatsoever.

## 7

quasquidem litteras et processus habitos per easdem et quecumque inde secuta ad dictam ecclesiam volumus non extendi sed nullum per hoc eis quo ad assecutionem benefitiorum aliorum preiuditium generari et quibuslibet privilegiis indulgentiis ac litteris apostolicis generalibus vel specialibus quorumcunque tenorum existant per que presentibus non expressa vel totaliter non inserta effectus earum impediri valeat quomodolibet vel differri et de quibus quorumque totis tenoribus habenda sit in nostris litteris mentio specialis proviso quod dicta ecclesia debitis propterea non fraudetur obsequiis et animarum cura in ea nullatenus negligatur.

Which letters, and the proceedings held through them, and whatever has followed therefrom, we will not to extend to the said church, yet no prejudice is to be created for them hereby with respect to the obtaining of other benefices; and notwithstanding any privileges, indulgences, and general or special apostolic letters, of whatever tenors they may be, through which—not being expressed or wholly inserted in these presents—the effect of these letters could in any way be hindered or delayed, and of which and of whose entire tenors special mention would have to be made in our letters; provided that the said church is not thereby defrauded of its due services and that the cure of souls in it is in no way neglected.

## 8

nos enim exnunc irritum decernimus et inane si secus super hiis a quoquam quavis auctoritate scienter vel ignoranter contigerit attemptari.

For we declare from now on to be null and void whatever may happen to be attempted to the contrary regarding these matters by anyone, by whatever authority, knowingly or unknowingly.

## 9

datum Basilee iiii idus aprilis anno a nativitate domini millesimoquadringentesimoquadragesimosecundo.

Given at Basel on the fourth day before the Ides of April, in the year from the Nativity of the Lord one thousand four hundred and forty-two.
