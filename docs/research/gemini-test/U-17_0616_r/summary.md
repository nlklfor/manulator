# Summary: U-17_0616_r (1442, Basel)

In Basel on 10 April 1442, the Council of Basel directed the Provost of St. Peter’s in Basel to investigate a petition from the abbess and convent of St. Clare in Königsfelden. King Frederick of the Romans had earlier granted them patronage of Schinznach’s parish church on an unstated date. After summoning Basel’s bishop and the parish rector and verifying the nuns' claims, the provost must permanently incorporate the church into the monastery, reserving support for a vicar.
