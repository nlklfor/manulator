# Summary: U-17_0234_r (1350, Basel)

Bishop John of Basel, with his chapter's consent and in consideration of Queen Agnes of Hungary, incorporates Erlinsbach parish church into Königsfelden monastery. Once the current rector resigns or dies, the abbess and nuns will receive its revenues to support their community. They must present a perpetual vicar to the bishop and reserve an augmented prebend for him. It was issued in Basel in 1350, on the Friday after St. Martin's feast.
